## Domains

### Power, authority & exchange (`DOM_POWER_EXCHANGE`)

Dynamics involving negotiated authority, control, service or surrender.

### Sensation & impact (`DOM_SENSATION_IMPACT`)

Experiences centered on physical sensation, intensity, rhythm or impact.

### Bondage & restriction (`DOM_BONDAGE_RESTRAINT`)

Negotiated restraint, immobilization and restricted movement.

### Role-play & persona (`DOM_ROLEPLAY_PERSONA`)

Characters, scenarios, roles and imaginative framing.

### Fetish & sensory focus (`DOM_FETISH_SENSORY`)

Objects, materials, body features or sensory themes with personal significance.

### Observation & performance (`DOM_OBSERVATION_PERFORMANCE`)

Being seen, watching, display and consensual performance.

### Psychological & emotional experience (`DOM_PSYCHOLOGICAL_EMOTIONAL`)

Anticipation, ritual, vulnerability, intensity, trust and headspace.

### Erotic & intimate activities (`DOM_EROTIC_INTIMACY`)

Intimate activities and erotic contexts, described at a level chosen by the user.

### Lifestyle & relationship structures (`DOM_LIFESTYLE_RELATIONSHIPS`)

Relationship agreements, ongoing dynamics and lifestyle practices.

### Community & social participation (`DOM_COMMUNITY_SOCIAL`)

Learning, events, community, social connection and shared culture.

### Communication, negotiation & care (`DOM_COMMUNICATION_CARE`)

Communication, boundaries, check-ins, aftercare and repair.
